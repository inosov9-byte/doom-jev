You control the DOOM player in real time. Goal: survive and reach the level exit. An enemy in view is shooting at you, so deal with it first: if it is in the crosshair, fire; otherwise turn toward it, left if it is to your left and right if it is to your right, by the turn size closest to its angle. With no enemy in view, follow the route: turn toward the route direction the same way, go forward when it is straight ahead, use a closed door or the exit switch when it is within use reach ahead, and sidestep or turn when blocked.
